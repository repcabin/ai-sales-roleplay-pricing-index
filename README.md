# AI Sales Roleplay Pricing Index (November 2026)

Which AI sales roleplay tools publish a price you can buy at, and which make you book a demo first. Every row comes from the vendor's own pricing or product page, checked on 2026-11-24. The full set of 289 sourced claims, each with its URL and check date, is in [vendor-facts.csv](vendor-facts.csv).

**Short answer:** of 21 AI sales roleplay and practice vendors checked, 14 publish no price for a team plan and route buyers to a demo or quote. 7 publish at least one paid price. Free trials and free tiers are common; public team prices are not.

## Method

1. One row per vendor that sells AI roleplay or AI practice for sales or service reps.
2. "Team price published" means a dollar price for a plan a sales team can buy, on the vendor's own site. A free tier alone does not count.
3. Claims are copied as stated by the vendor on the check date. Prices change; open the source link before you buy.
4. Corrections: open an issue with the vendor URL that shows the change.
